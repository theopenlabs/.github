# Open Labs

Open-source alternatives to overpriced professional software.

Some professional tools cost tens of thousands of dollars a year, even though much of the data underneath is public.
We rebuild them in the open: every fact links to its source, you can host everything yourself, and each product
works inside AI assistants such as Claude through MCP.

## Products

| Product | What it does | Status |
|---|---|---|
| [OpenLandscape](https://openlandscape.app) | Drug-pipeline landscapes from ClinicalTrials.gov and FDA data, for people and AI agents. An open alternative to Citeline and Evaluate. | Early access, Nov 2026 |

## How we build

- **Sourced.** Every number links to the record it came from. Unconfirmed facts are marked.
- **Open.** Servers are AGPL-3.0; clients and skills are MIT. Self-hosting is free.
- **Agent-native.** Each product ships an MCP server, so you can ask your assistant instead of clicking through tables.
- **Fair pricing.** A free tier, a hosted plan for people who would rather not run servers, and no sales calls.

Questions or a product you want rebuilt in the open: open an issue on the product's repository.
